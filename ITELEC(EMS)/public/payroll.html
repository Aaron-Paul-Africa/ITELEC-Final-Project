Working hours, overtime, overtime pay, and deductions are recalculated from each attendance date and its time-in/time-out. Net salary = ₱25,000 basic salary + overtime pay − late deductions − absence deductions. Each missed weekday, including a zero-time absence record, is 8 hours × ₱300 = ₱2,400. Weekends are not deducted; current-month absences are counted through today.
